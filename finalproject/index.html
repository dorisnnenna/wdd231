DNA Frozens & Condiments

Website Project Site Plan

1. Site Name

DNA Frozens & Condiments

The name represents a food business that specializes in frozen foods and carefully packaged condiments. "DNA" is used as the brand identity, while "Frozens & Condiments" clearly describes the products offered by the business.

2. Site Purpose

The website will introduce DNA Frozens & Condiments and provide customers with information about available frozen foods and condiments, including product details, prices, and delivery services. The website will allow visitors to explore available products and submit an order or inquiry through an online form.

3. Scenarios

The following questions represent common questions that customers may have when visiting the website:

  • What frozen foods and condiments are available from DNA Frozens & Condiments, and how much do they cost?
  • Does DNA Frozens & Condiments offer delivery, and how can I place an order?
  • What sizes or quantities are available for the different products?

4. Color Scheme

The color scheme is designed to give the website a clean, sophisticated, and premium food-brand appearance.

Deep Green — #173F35

Used for the header, navigation, major headings, buttons, and other important brand elements.

Cream — #F8F5ED

Used as the primary background color to create a warm, clean, and welcoming appearance.

Gold — #C49A4A

Used as an accent color for highlights, borders, decorative elements, and selected call-to-action elements.

5. Typography

Playfair Display

Playfair Display will be used for major headings, the business name, and prominent hero text. It provides an elegant and sophisticated appearance.

Poppins

Poppins will be used for body text, navigation links, buttons, product information, and other interface elements. It provides a clean and modern appearance.

6. Wireframe

The home page wireframe shows the basic structure and layout of the website for both small and large screen sizes. The wireframe focuses on the arrangement of content rather than colors, images, fonts, and detailed styling.

Small Viewport

The mobile layout includes a responsive header with a hamburger navigation menu, a hero section, featured products, delivery information, an introduction to the business, and a footer.

Large Viewport

The desktop layout uses the available screen width to arrange the navigation horizontally and display featured products and other content in wider sections or columns.
